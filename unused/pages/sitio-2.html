What We Do — Page Introduction

From dispersed sources to a system your team can use and maintain.

We work with clients to organize business knowledge, connect the sources required for a specific task, and create repeatable AI-assisted workflows.

The result is not a generic chatbot or a demonstration that depends on consultants. It is a bounded working system, tested with the people who will use it and designed for independent operation.

Architecture — The System
01

Sources

The documents, datasets, reports, platforms, and expert knowledge required for the selected business question. Sources remain subject to the client’s access rules and approved environment.

02

Knowledge channels

A maintainable layer that gives scattered information shared meaning. A channel can include definitions, source references, ownership, permissions, freshness, instructions, and review rules for one business topic.

03

Reusable skills

Checked procedures for recurring work: what the task is, which sources to use, which calculations or tools are permitted, what the output should contain, and how the result is reviewed.

04

Agents and tools

An AI agent uses the approved context and procedures to carry out bounded work. Where exact figures matter, it uses a validated query or calculation rather than guessing from retrieved text.

05

Shared outputs

Reports, dashboards, summaries, evidence, and open questions produced in a consistent form that authorized employees can understand and reuse.

06

Human review

A person checks the result, applies judgment, and remains accountable. A responsible owner reviews changes before they become shared guidance.

Methodology — Our Client Workflow
Phase I

Assess the opportunity

We begin with a paid readiness assessment. Together we choose one recurring decision or task, inspect the source material, identify owners and participants, understand access constraints, and define what a useful result must demonstrate.

Phase II

Build the workflow

We prepare the agreed knowledge package and configure the retrieval, skills, tools, and agent workflow required for that use case. We use existing provider capabilities where they are sufficient and add custom work only when the evidence justifies it.

Phase III

Test with the team

Participants use representative questions and compare the outputs with agreed answers, evidence, and calculations. We test incomplete, stale, conflicting, and denied-access cases. A second participant should be able to repeat and explain the work.

Phase IV

Hand over and maintain

We transfer the client-specific assets, configuration, instructions, evaluation cases, update process, and cost-monitoring guidance. Exact ownership, licensing, provider, and infrastructure terms are agreed for each engagement.
