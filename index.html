PRINCIPLES

A useful system keeps people in the loop.

01

Trusted knowledge

Use approved sources, shared definitions, and visible evidence. When information is incomplete or conflicting, the system should say so.

02

Repeatable work

Turn proven methods into reusable instructions, tools, and checks so good work can travel across people and teams.

03

Human judgment

AI supports the work. People remain responsible for interpretation, approval, and important decisions.

THE CLIENT WORKFLOW

How an engagement works.

01

Assess

Choose one recurring business question. Review the relevant sources, definitions, people, access requirements, current effort, and operating costs.

02

Build together

Organize the knowledge, configure the retrieval and tools the workflow actually needs, and create a bounded agent or process with the client team.

03

Test and practice

Run representative questions, compare results with approved sources or calculations, test failure cases, and practice with real participants.

04

Hand over

Transfer the working assets, maintenance guidance, evaluation cases, and cost controls so the client can operate and improve the system.
